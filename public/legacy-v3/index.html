Safety & Inclusivity Charter

Bandhan is built on absolute respect, physical safety, gender equality, and zero tolerance for harassment.

✅

Encouraged & Celebrated

  • Warm, Respectful Public Demeanor: Holding hands for photos, linking arms while entering events, smiling, touching elders' feet, and dancing gracefully at Sangeets.
  • Full Inclusivity: Guy, Girl, Transgender Women, Transgender Men, Non-Binary & Queer individuals are treated with equal honor, dignity, and preferred pronouns.
  • Authentic Family Impressions: Polite dining etiquette, helping with elders, engaging in charming conversation with relatives, colleagues, and bosses.
  • Discreet Privacy: Absolute confidentiality. No personal details or rental contracts are ever shared with third parties.
🚫

Strictly Prohibited (Zero Tolerance)

  • Zero Physical Intimacy: Companion rentals are 100% platonic. No kissing, overnight hotel room stays, or sexual solicitations. Violations result in immediate police reporting and blacklisting.
  • No Forced Misgendering or Harassment: Any transphobic, queerphobic, sexist, casteist, or degrading remarks result in instant SOS intervention.
  • No Intoxicated Misbehavior: Both client and companion agree to drink responsibly. Excessive intoxication that causes public embarrassment is forbidden.
  • No Financial Extortion: No unauthorized cash demands, tips, or off-platform side-deals. Everything is governed transparently via ₹ INR on Bandhan.

  • Every profile in this prototype uses illustrative verification data; no government-ID KYC or real-world identity verification is performed.

  • Transgender and queer community members have designated safety guardians and priority incident resolution teams.
